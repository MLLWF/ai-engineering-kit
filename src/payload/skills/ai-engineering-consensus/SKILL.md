Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time. After reaching engineering consensus on a branch with me, first record or update the fact according to `docs/ai-engineering/fact-record-spec.md`, then continue.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase and the fact record repository, explore the codebase and the fact record repository instead.
